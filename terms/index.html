Last updated: November 11, 2026

Your People is a private online home for church congregations, operated from Nova Scotia, Canada ("we", "the app"). By creating an account or joining a church group, you agree to these terms.

Accounts and groups

An account needs a real name and email address. Members join a church with its join code, and a church's leaders may need to approve new members before they can take part. Leaders are responsible for the groups they create: for who they approve, and for the life of their congregation in them. Your People provides tools; it does not run any church, and leaders and their churches remain responsible for their own communities.

Acceptable use

Members may not bully, threaten, or harass anyone; post sexual content; or use the app for anything unlawful. That applies in direct messages just as much as in the open group. You can block any member and stop their messages reaching you. Leaders may remove members, and we may suspend or close accounts or groups that break these terms.

Direct messages

Members of the same church can message each other one to one. Those conversations are between the two people in them: a church's leaders cannot read other members' direct messages, and neither do we, outside of keeping the service running and acting on reports. If you delete your account, your direct-message conversations are deleted with it.

Screening and moderation

Questions sent to a church's leaders are screened automatically for signs that someone may be thinking of harming themselves; a question that matches is held for the church's leaders, so a person — not a program — decides how to respond. Open group chat is conversation between adults and is not screened. Screening is a support for leaders; it cannot catch everything, and a held question is never proof of anything about a person. Leaders can also remove content and members at any time.

Your content

What you post stays yours. You give us permission to store and display it to the people it was posted for, for as long as your account or the group keeps it. When you delete your account, your posts are deleted with it (see our account deletion page).

Giving and tips

A church's giving button opens that church's own CanadaHelps or Tithely page; those gifts are between you and that church, and the app does not process them. The app's tip jar is a voluntary gift to the app's creator, processed by Stripe. A tip is not a purchase and does not unlock anything; the app keeps no percentage of it. If a tip was made by mistake, email us and we will refund it.
