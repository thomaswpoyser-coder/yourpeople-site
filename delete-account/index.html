Delete your account

You can delete your Your People account, and the personal data attached to it, at any time. There are two ways:

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Your People and tap the small gear at the top right to open Settings
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account
  3. Type DELETE to confirm

Your account, profile and sign-in are removed right away, and so is what you posted — your group chat messages, your direct messages and conversations, your questions and prayer requests, and the sermons or lessons you posted are deleted with the account. Your directory entry and any directory photo you uploaded are removed too.

By email

If you can't sign in, email youth@cbckc.ca from the email address on the account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm once it is done, within 30 days at the latest and usually much sooner.

If you created a church group, deleting your account does not delete the group — it keeps running for its members, but it loses the person who created it, so hand leadership to another leader inside the app first if you want it to keep one. Events and announcements belong to the group rather than to you, so they stay. Questions: youth@cbckc.ca. See also our privacy policy.
